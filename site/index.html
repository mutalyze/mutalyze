How it works

Everything it checks. Nothing it fakes.

Compile your rules once, then execute against the log — deterministically. Every finding is a fact you can open the transcript and verify.

Branch-safe · turn-indexed

One pass. Correct turn numbers across rewinds & subagents.

ruleguard traces the main path through the transcript and pins every finding to a real turn — so a citation always resolves to the exact line.

Honest by default

What it won't do.

The instrument is only worth trusting if it's straight about its own edges. These are in the README, not the fine print.

✗
Checkable rules skew trivial.

The rules easy to check mechanically usually aren't the ones that matter. Those get marked unsupported — counted, never faked.

✗
“Secrets to disk” is scoped.

It catches a key written to .env or id_rsa — not one hard-coded into config.ts. Not a full secret scanner.

✗
A quiet run isn't a clean bill of health.

Zero findings can mean compliance — or that your rules compiled to unsupported. Read the counts, not just the violation number.
